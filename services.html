How the Reset Unfolds

Structured. Confidential. Field-tested.

Every Reset runs on a deliberate cadence. No improvisation, no fluff. Here is what the quarter actually looks like.

Days 1 to 14

Diagnose the real constraint

Most operators are working hard on the wrong problem. A confidential, unflinching diagnosis of where the genuine constraint lives — in the leader, the model, or the rhythm. Nothing is prescribed until the constraint is named.

Days 15 to 45

Rebuild the operating model

We redesign how decisions get made, where the leader's attention goes, and which systems carry weight the founder currently carries alone — removing the founder as the bottleneck without removing them from what matters.

Days 46 to 90

Install recovery, re-enter compounding

Energy and the long-game rhythm are engineered in before we push for more output. With the constraint cleared and the operator protected, decision velocity returns and outcomes begin to compound.
