Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly will I hear back?

All inquiries receive an initial response within 24-48 hours during business hours (Monday-Friday, 9am-6pm EST).

Is the discovery call really free?

Yes, the 30-minute discovery call is completely complimentary with no obligation to continue coaching.

What should I prepare for the discovery call?

Think about your current goals, challenges, and what you hope to achieve through coaching. No formal preparation needed.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes, we work with clients globally via video conference. Sessions are scheduled according to your timezone.

What's the investment for coaching?

Coaching programs range from $7,500 to $25,000 for 90-day engagements, depending on the program and level of support.

Can I book a single session?

While we focus on transformative 90-day engagements, single strategy sessions are available starting at $2,500.
