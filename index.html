The Honest Diagnostic

Where most senior executives quietly plateau

Five patterns show up consistently in the leaders who arrive in my coaching room. Most recognize at least two before naming any of them out loud.

01

The founder is still the bottleneck

The team is empowered until something matters. Then they wait for you. The business has scaled. The operating model has not.

02

Decision fatigue at the top

By 4 PM, the same person who made sharp calls at 9 AM is rounding off the edges of bigger decisions. Fewer decisions, sharper outcomes — that's the lever.

03

Recovery has stopped working

A weekend used to refresh. A vacation used to reset. When neither does, the system has been depleted longer than you realize.

04

The strategic constraint is hiding in plain sight

Most plateaued leaders have one constraint they've worked around for 18–36 months. They know it's there. The plateau persists because addressing it is uncomfortable.

05

Identity merged with the role

Every win produces relief, not satisfaction. Every setback feels personal in a way that's out of proportion. The fusion is the diagnosis.
